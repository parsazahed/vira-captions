Vira is ready to open.

  1. Quit and reopen Premiere Pro.
  2. Choose Window → Extensions → Vira Captions. The menu may say Extensions (Legacy).
  3. Connect your speech service, choose your video and language, then click Generate subtitles.

Your provider API key is a secret code from the speech service. Vira asks for it in the panel, never in this installer.

This preview is not certified for every Premiere version. If the panel does not appear, check the help page in the project README.
