Welcome to Vira · ویرا

Subtitles for your videos, with English and Persian support.

This installer puts Vira in the correct Adobe folder for your Mac account. You do not need Terminal, Node.js, or a build command.

  1. Click Continue and follow the installation steps.
  2. Quit and reopen Premiere Pro.
  3. Choose Window → Extensions → Vira Captions.
  4. In Vira, click Connect your speech service and paste your API key.

Vira looks for Premiere's built-in audio settings automatically. There are no audio presets to create.

About this preview
This is an unsigned development preview for Apple Silicon Macs. Installing it enables Adobe's unsigned-extension mode for your account (CEP 11 and 12). This allows other unsigned CEP extensions to load too. Continue only if you accept that change. The installer does not alter macOS Gatekeeper. Premiere integration still needs testing in the actual application.
